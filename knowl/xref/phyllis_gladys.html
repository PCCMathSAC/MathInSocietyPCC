Example 2.5.13.

Phyllis and Gladys are married and filing jointly. Together their taxable income is $132,000. Use the simplified 2024 tax tables from this section to determine how much they owe in taxes.
🔗
Solution.
Since Phyllis and Gladys are married and filing jointly, their taxable income puts them in the 22% tax bracket. Using the simplified 2024 tax table, their taxes are $10,852 plus 22% of the excess over $94,300:
🔗
\begin{align*} \$10{,}852+0.22(\$132{,}000-\$94{,}300)\amp=\$10{,}852+0.22(\$37{,}700)\\ \amp=\$10{,}852+\$8{,}294\\ \amp=\$19{,}146 \end{align*}
🔗
Phyllis and Gladys owe $19,146 in taxes.
